About

I am an Economist in the Directorate General Monetary Policy at the European Central Bank. My research interests include macro-finance, monetary policy, and international economics. I received a PhD in Economics from King's College London. I also completed the ECB Graduate Programme.

Before joining the ECB, I worked as a Stipendiary Fellow in Economics at the University of Edinburgh.
