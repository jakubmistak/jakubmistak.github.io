Welcome to my research website

I am an Economist in the Directorate General Monetary Policy (DG-MP) at the European Central Bank. My research interests include macro-finance, monetary economics, and international economics. I previously completed the ECB Graduate Programme.

I hold a PhD in Economics from King’s College London. During my PhD studies, I was a PhD Trainee in the Directorate General Research (DG-R) at the ECB.

Before completing my PhD, I was also a full-time Stipendiary Fellow in Economics at the University of Edinburgh School of Economics and held Teaching Assistant positions at King’s College London and the University of York.
