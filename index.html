About

I am an Economist in the Directorate General Monetary Policy (DGMP) at the European Central Bank (ECB). I previously completed the ECB Graduate Programme. I hold a PhD in Economics from King’s College London, with a focus on Macro-Finance and International Economics. During my PhD studies, I was a PhD Trainee in the Directorate General Research (DGR) at the ECB. Before completing my PhD, I was also a full-time Stipendiary Fellow in Economics at the University of Edinburgh School of Economics and held Teaching Assistant positions at King’s College London and the University of York.

Before joining the ECB, I worked as a Stipendiary Fellow in Economics at the University of Edinburgh.
